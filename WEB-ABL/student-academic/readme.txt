### 3. Student Academic Dashboard

**Files:** `student_academic` and its `style.css`

A dashboard where a student can see their college details in one place.

What is on the page:
- A side menu (Dashboard, My Subjects, Results, Attendance, Exams)
- Student details: name, roll number, and semester
- Attendance bars for Python, Mathematics, and Web Development
- A table with subject, faculty name, and marks
- The exam schedule
- Announcements for students
