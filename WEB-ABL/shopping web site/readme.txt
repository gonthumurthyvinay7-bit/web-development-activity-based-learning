### 2. ShopEasy (Shopping Website)

**Files:** `shopping_web` and its `style.css`

A simple online shop home page.

What is on the page:
- A header with menu links (Home, Products, Categories, Cart)
- A sale banner: "Big Sale - Up to 50% OFF!"
- Four product cards: Headphones, Smart Watch, Sports Shoes, and Backpack
- Each card shows the category, a short detail, a star rating, the old and new price in ₹, the discount, and a "Buy Now" button
- A footer
